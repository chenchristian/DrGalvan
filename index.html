Meet your dentist

Dr. Steve Galvan, DDS

Dr. Steve Galvan started his dental education at Centro Escolar University, Manila, Philippines and graduated in 1988, becoming a third generation dentist. In October 1995, Dr. Galvan started his own practice in Fairfield, CA and three years later opened his second office in Concord, CA. Since then, he has provided beautiful smiles to patients residing in Solano County, Contra Costa County, and Napa County.

  • 1988Graduated, Centro Escolar University, Manila
